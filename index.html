LogoLab — free online image to SVG vectorizer

Convert a PNG, JPG or WebP logo or icon into a clean, editable SVG, right in your browser. No upload, no sign-up, no watermark, free and open source (MIT).

  • Vectorize: real tracing (region segmentation and curve fitting), not an image embedded in an SVG. Flat colour, one-ink and gradient art; line art comes back as strokes.
  • Clean up: remove a baked-in background with a flood fill, brushes or an in-browser AI cutout.
  • Edit: a built-in SVG editor with node editing, booleans and live text.
  • Preview: see the logo on devices, websites, browser tabs, app stores and avatars, from 16px up.
  • Export: favicons, apple-touch, PWA and Android icon sets; split an AI-generated icon sheet into separate icons.

The same vectorizer runs from coding agents as an MCP server: npx -y logolab. Source: github.com/Blaxzter/LogoLab.
